❓ How to play

Survive 10 waves of haters to get elected. Your candidate shoots automatically. Collect 🔵 votes to level up and pick upgrades, including 4 bonus weapons. Bosses show up on waves 5 and 10, and Endless mode adds 2 more.

🔥 Combo: keep killing to build a vote multiplier, up to x3. Getting hit resets it.
✨ Elites: golden haters. Tougher, but they drop lots of votes and power-ups.
🎲 Wave events: random modifiers like Golden Hour, Debate Night or Blackout.
💰 Funds: earned every run. Spend them in 🏛️ Campaign HQ on permanent upgrades.

⌨️ Keyboard + Mouse

  • Move: WASD or Arrow keys
  • Special: Space, E, or Right-click
  • Dodge roll: Shift or Q (invincible while rolling)
  • Aim: automatic, or the mouse if Manual aim is on in Settings
  • Upgrades: 1, 2, 3 or click
  • Pause: P or Esc · Mute: M · Fullscreen: F

📱 Touch

  • Move: the joystick bottom-left (or drag anywhere on the left half)
  • Special: big round button, bottom right
  • Dodge roll: 💨 button next to it
  • Aim: automatic, or drag on the right half if Manual aim is on
  • Pause: ⏸ top right

🎮 Gamepad

  • Move: Left stick or D-pad
  • Aim: Right stick (otherwise auto)
  • Special: A or RB/RT
  • Dodge roll: B or LB/LT
  • Pause: Start · Back: B
  • Menus: stick/D-pad + A
