❓ How to play

  1. 🪂 Battle Royale (Classic / Ranked): jump from the plane, loot guns and gear, stay inside the shrinking zone, and be the last squad standing for the BOOYAH. Squad and Duo come with bot teammates.
  2. ⚔️ Clash Squad: 4v4 rounds in a small arena. Each round starts with a 10 s buy phase (🛒). Earn coins from kills and rounds, and win 4 rounds to take the match.
  3. 🩸 Knock & revive: in team modes a downed player crawls and bleeds out. Stand next to a knocked teammate and hold E / ➕ REVIVE to bring them back.
  4. 🧊 Gloo walls: instant cover that blocks bullets until it's shot down. Use them!
  5. 🦸 Skills & pets: equip 1 active skill (SKILL button), 2 passive skills and a pet in the lobby. Buy more with 🪙 coins and 💎 diamonds earned by playing.
  6. 🏆 Ranks: Ranked modes give rank points: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Heroic → Grandmaster.
