❓ How to play

  1. 🛫 Drop: everyone flies over the island. Hit JUMP when you're over a spot you like. Your parachute opens on its own, or press Jump again to open it early.
  2. 🔫 Loot: run into buildings and around crates. Grab guns (✋ pick up), ammo, 🦺 vests, ⛑️ helmets, 🩹 bandages, 🧰 med kits, 🥤 drinks and 💣 grenades. Ammo, heals and armor upgrades are picked up automatically.
  3. 🔵 Zone: the blue wall shrinks over time. Outside it you lose HP, and it gets worse every phase. Watch the timer and the map.
  4. 🏆 Win: be the last of 50 candidates standing. Headshots do extra damage, and helmets reduce it.

📱 Phone

  • Left side: drag to move (push far to sprint)
  • Right side: drag to look
  • 🔥 Fire. Hold it and drag to aim while shooting. There's a 2nd fire button on the left.
  • 🎯 Scope · ⤒ Jump · ⤓ Crouch · ↻ Reload
  • 🩹 Heal · 🥤 Drink · 💣 Grenade · tap a weapon slot to switch

⌨️ Keyboard + Mouse

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch
  • Mouse look (click the game to lock the mouse) · LMB fire · RMB scope
  • R reload · E/F pick up · 1/2 or wheel: weapons · X holster
  • H heal · B drink · G grenade · M/Tab map · Esc pause

🎮 Controller

  • Left stick move (click to sprint) · Right stick look
  • RT fire · LT scope · A jump · B crouch
  • X reload / pick up · Y switch weapon
  • LB grenade · RB heal · View map · Menu pause
